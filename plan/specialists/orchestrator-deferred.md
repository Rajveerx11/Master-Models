# Orchestrator — deferred

The orchestrator is not a sixth synthetic specialist.

Start this work only when at least three domain specialists beat stock Qwen3-4B on
their frozen gates. Collect real routing logs containing request, available specialists,
chosen route, outcome, fallback, and human correction.

Do not generate routing labels from domain keywords alone. Use a simple deterministic
router until enough real failures exist to justify training. Cross-domain tasks fall
back to the general model unless one specialist can own the whole task.
